National, by 2035

Inclusive volunteering is recognised as a working instrument of the SDGs and the NDS30.

What this depends on

This chain holds as long as three conditions are met: the legal framework for volunteering set by Law No 2021/015 remains in force; volunteers stay engaged through mentoring, recognition and reimbursement of costs; and partners sustain the co-funding that lets trained young people put their skills to work.

Four programme areas

Nine domains of intervention are set out in our statutes. In practice, the work concentrates in four areas where volunteers deliver measurable results.

Youth livelihoods

Training in creative, innovative and income-generating activities, including locally manufactured products such as bleach and liquid soap.

Volpreneurship — 100+ trained in 2025

Climate and environment

School climate campaigns, tree planting, landscape restoration and clean-up operations, largely through the YONECA network.

5 schools reached in 2024–2025

Civic engagement

Volunteer deployment to national civic platforms: Youth Day, the National Youth Camp, the National Citizenship Games and the National Unity Village.

50+ volunteers served at the 2025 National Youth Camp

Humanitarian action and inclusion

Support to vulnerable groups, community health education, and participation in national and Francophone civil society coordination.

45+ CSOs convened with MINREX in July 2025

Where we work

Operations are coordinated from Yaoundé, with volunteers mobilised through monthly meetings held online and in person.

  • Head office Yaoundé, Mfoundi Division, Centre Region.
  • Centre Regional coordination led by Fadimatou Aya Ousmanou, also Deputy Secretary General of the YONECA network.
  • National Volunteers drawn from all ten regions have taken part in national events convened by MINJEC and CONEP.

Deployment capacity

Our volunteer database holds more than 500 records. Volunteers have served in technical secretariat, protocol and security, catering, health and volunteer-coordination roles at national scale, and hold certification from the National Volunteering Programme.

Partner with us

We work as an implementing partner for international organisations, as a consortium member, and directly with ministries and councils. What we bring to a programme:

  • A registered non-profit with continuous legal existence since November 2022
  • 500+ volunteers, coordinated monthly, deployable at short notice
  • Bilingual delivery and reporting in English and French
  • A dedicated MEAL and translation unit within the executive structure
  • Dual-signature control on all disbursements, per our statutes
  • Established access to ministries, schools and national youth platforms

Get involved

Two ways to join: serve as a volunteer, or become a voting member of the association.

Volunteer

Volunteering with CIVASS means serving without wage, and being trained while you do it. You join a network of more than 500 people, receive capacity-building and mentoring, and represent CIVASS in the field and at national events.

Apply to volunteer

Become a member

Members vote, stand for election and take part in the General Assembly, which meets in plenary session on the last Saturday of each month.

Registration: 5,000 FCFA, non-refundable. Annual dues: 10,000 FCFA.
